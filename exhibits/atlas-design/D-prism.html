03 The ask

What you can ask it to watch.

Atlas points outward. Name a thing in the world and the sentence you would use to check on it. It is not a flowchart builder and not an integrations marketplace — there is nothing to connect.

If you can say it in a sentence, Atlas can watch it.

It watches things outside your accounts, not inside them. It cannot read your inbox or your connected apps.

04 How it works

A sentence goes in whole. What comes back is separated.

Three beats, and nothing to configure between them.

Beat 1 — In

The sentence goes in

One English sentence carrying both halves: what you want, and when you want it. There is no second screen. The sentence is the task.

Every morning, tell me the forecast for Boston — rain, temperature, and whether I need a coat.

Schedule daily 07:00 Prompt the rest of it

The two halves are read through different filters, so they leave the sentence slightly displaced from one another.

Plain English is not ours alone — Zapier Copilot ships it free. It is only the honest way in.

Beat 2 — Worked

The schedule is parsed

“Every morning” resolves to daily 07:00. Ten frequencies exist, and the split between them is a paywall, so here it is plainly.

  • daily
  • weekdays
  • weekly
  • once
  • 30m
  • 1h
  • 2h

  • 12h

Free's floor is daily. Sub-daily is a Pro feature, and that is a real limit: Zapier and Make will poll every fifteen minutes on their free tiers. Atlas will not. There is no monthly schedule on any plan.

Model — Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite, included, no key required. Or bring your own key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Beat 3 — Out

The result is delivered

Every run is graded before it reaches you. Six quality tags; after three flagged runs in a row the task pauses itself rather than keep spending your allowance.

Re: Boston morning forecast — run 001

High 68°F, low 54°F. Rain likely 2pm–6pm, 70% chance — take the coat. Wind 12mph NE. Sunrise 05:31.

checked: has a source, nothing missing

Email arrives as a daily digest on Free, at an hour you pick — every run as it lands is Pro. Browser push and anomaly alerts either way.

05 The check

Every run is graded before it reaches you.

This is how these tools fail quietly: a confident number arrives in the same email, at the same time, looking exactly like a good one. So Atlas grades every run before it reaches you — blank replies, refusals, answers cut off mid-sentence, claims with no source. Each gets its own tag in your run history.

The filter stack

empty

the reply comes back blank

refusal

the model declines to answer

truncated

the answer stops mid-sentence

loop

it repeats itself and stalls

unsourced

a claim with no source — run B stops here

ok

nothing caught it — it reaches you

run A ok run B unsourced

Two runs go in at the left, and they look identical on the way in — that is the whole problem. Run A passes all six filters and leaves the far side tagged ok. Run B carries a claim with no source: it travels normally until the fifth filter and is stopped there, tagged unsourced. Nothing continues past that plate.

Three strikes, then it stops

A task that keeps failing pauses itself. Three consecutive flagged runs, then it stops — it does not keep spending your runs on an answer that isn't arriving.

Auto-pause notice — boston-morning-forecast — paused after 3 consecutive flagged runs.

You hear about it once a week at most — one click snoozes it for thirty days, and the first good run clears the flag on its own. Nothing here nags.

flag 1 flag 2 flag 3

A density wedge for each consecutive flagged run. The third is fully dense, and the bar after it is where the task stops itself.

07 Comparison

Atlas is a narrow filter.

Zapier, Make and n8n are visual editors with hundreds of connectors, and all three ship AI assistance now. Atlas starts from the other end: you describe the outcome, and the model works out how. Less setup for open-ended, judgement-heavy work — and far fewer integrations than any of them. If your automation is a fixed chain between two specific apps, they are the better tool. If it is watch this and tell me what matters, that is this one.
