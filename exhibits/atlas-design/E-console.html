05 Quality interlock TOL ±0.02

This is how these tools fail quietly.

A confident number arrives in the same email, at the same time, looking exactly like a good one. So Atlas grades every run before it reaches you — blank replies, refusals, answers cut off mid-sentence, claims with no source. Each gets its own tag in your run history.

Six tags exist. After three consecutive flagged runs the task stops itself, and the notice tells you which tag tripped it. This is the one thing the other tools do not do.

Quality annunciator task boston-morning-forecast · run 023

  • empty blank reply
  • refusal model declined
  • truncated cut off mid-sentence
  • loop repeats itself
  • unsourced claim with no source
Fault register
run n−2 run n−1 run n

Three flagged runs in a row. Limit 3. On the third, Atlas stops the task rather than sending you a fourth answer it does not trust.

Run enable

Interlock tripped

The guard is up and the switch is safed. Nothing runs again on this task until you resume it — or until you snooze the whole notice for thirty days.

Auto-pause notice

boston-morning-forecast — paused after 3 consecutive flagged runs.

You hear about it once a week at most — one click snoozes it for thirty days, and the first good run clears the flag on its own. Nothing here nags.

07 System compare M4 × 0.7

Where Atlas fits, and where it doesn’t.

Zapier and Make are visual editors with large connector libraries, and both now ship AI assistance too. Atlas starts from the other end: describe the outcome, AI works out how. Less setup for open-ended, judgement-heavy tasks — and, honestly, far fewer integrations than they have.

If your automation is a fixed chain between specific apps, they are the better fit. If it is “watch this and tell me what matters,” that is Atlas.

The bat only goes one way

Atlas is built in the top position and will not throw into the bottom one. That is a decision, not a roadmap item — the whole product is a sentence, a schedule and a grade, so a job that is really a chain of specific app steps has nothing here to hold on to.

Stated plainly, so it is not buried

  • Zapier and Make free tiers poll every 15 minutes. The Atlas free floor is daily.
  • 40 runs a month is the smallest free allowance in the category.
  • Plain-English setup is not unique to Atlas. Zapier Copilot ships it free.
  • No public API. No mobile app. No SSO. No monthly schedule.
