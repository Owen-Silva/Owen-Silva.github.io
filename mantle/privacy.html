Mantle privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Mantle is a desktop shell for Windows (taskbar, quick settings, calendar and similar tools) built for personal use. It runs entirely on your own PC. This page explains what Mantle does with your data when you connect Google Calendar.

What Mantle asks Google for

Mantle asks for one permission: read-only access to your calendars (calendar.readonly). It cannot create, change or delete events, and it does not ask for your email, contacts, Drive or anything else.

How the data is used

Mantle uses your calendar data only to show your events in its own calendar: a dot on days that have events, and a list of the selected day's events. Mantle's Settings also show which Google account is connected and the names of your calendars, so you can choose which ones appear. Nothing else.

Where the data is kept

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your PC, encrypted by Windows for your user account.
  • A copy of recent events is kept only on your PC, so the calendar opens instantly and still shows events when you are offline.

What is shared

Nothing. Mantle has no servers of its own and no analytics, advertising or tracking. It talks only to Google's servers, to read your calendar. Your calendar data is never sold, shared with anyone, or used for any other purpose.

Removing access

"Sign out" in Mantle's Settings deletes the token and cached events from your PC and asks Google to revoke Mantle's access. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mantle's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
