01 — Running here

Keeping only the frames where the slide changed

The lesson recorder keeps a frame only when the slide really changed, by reducing every frame to a 24 × 24 grayscale fingerprint and comparing it with the last frame it kept. The sequence below is drawn live in this page, with a clock that ticks on every frame and a background that drifts — so you can watch the detector refuse to be fooled by either.

The frame

Its 24 × 24 fingerprint

Every frame, and what was decided

    Mean absolute difference between fingerprints. Measured here: drift and the clock stay under 0.2, a slide change scores about 4 to 5, so the cut is 1.5.

    02 — Playable

    Star Wars: Strategic Battle

    A browser strategy game where you deploy Rebel or Imperial ships against an AI opponent. X-wings, B-wings, TIE fighters and Star Destroyers, with an upgrade system and a canvas animation loop, all in one 1,146-line file. Built in a day, 13 April 2026.

    It is mouse-only — there is no keyboard path through the game, so it will not be usable from the keyboard alone. It loads only when you press play.
